About

Hi, I'm
Danielle.

A marketer, designer, historian, and quiet observer of the universe.

I grew up between Southern California and North Carolina. Today, I reside in not-so-sunny San Francisco. (I still say y'all.) I've lived in both Paris and Tel Aviv, and I travel as often as I can.

Over my career I've shaped employer brands, launched careers sites, produced events with thousands of attendees, and built communications programs at some of the most interesting companies in tech — Airtable, Sony, Samsara, WeWork, Marqeta, and more.

Other interests include interior design, vintage cars, typography, cloud watching, and stargazing.
